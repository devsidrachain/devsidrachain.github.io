Security & User Safety

Protect your wallet and verify every interaction.

Stay Safe in Web3

  • Never share your wallet recovery phrase or private key.
  • Verify website addresses before connecting your wallet.
  • Read transaction details before signing or approving.
  • Do not interact with a website if your wallet flags it as unsafe.

A secure HTTPS connection alone does not guarantee that a website or smart contract is trustworthy.
